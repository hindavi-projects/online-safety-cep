Social Media Safety

Use social media confidently while keeping your personal information safe.

🔒

Use Privacy Settings

Set your account to private and decide who can see your posts and photos.

👥

Accept Known People Only

Do not accept friend requests from strangers.

📵

Think Before Posting

Once something is posted, it can be copied or shared.

🚫

Block and Report

Block and report harmful people or messages.

📍

Turn Off Live Location

Do not share your live location publicly.

🗣️

Ask for Help

Take a screenshot and tell a trusted adult if a message worries you.
