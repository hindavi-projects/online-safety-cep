Important Online Safety Tips

Follow these habits every time you use the internet.

✅ Use strong passwords: Use letters, numbers, and symbols. Do not use your name or birth date.
✅ Never share OTP or PIN: No bank, company, or genuine person will ask for your OTP.
✅ Check links first: Do not click unfamiliar links in emails, SMS, or social-media messages.
✅ Use two-step verification: It gives your account extra protection.
✅ Update your device: Updates fix security problems in phones, computers, browsers, and applications.
✅ Download safely: Install apps only from official stores such as Google Play Store.
✅ Think before posting: Do not post your address, phone number, school details, or live location.
✅ Tell a trusted adult: Tell a parent, teacher, guardian, or trusted adult if something online makes you worried.
