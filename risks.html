Common Online Risks

Learn how to identify online dangers by looking at simple examples.

🎣

Phishing

Phishing is a fake email, message, call, or website that tries to steal your password, OTP, or bank information.

Example: “Your bank account will be blocked today. Click here and enter your OTP.”

What to do: Do not click the link or share OTP. Open the official bank app or website yourself.

👤

Fake Profiles

Some strangers create false accounts and pretend to be friends, celebrities, or trusted people.

Example: A new account sends a message: “Hi, I am your school friend. Send me your phone number.”

What to do: Check whether you know the person in real life. Do not accept unknown friend requests.

⚠️

Online Scams

Scammers offer fake prizes, jobs, loans, discounts, or investments to take money or personal details.

Example: “Congratulations! You won ₹50,000. Pay ₹500 first to receive your prize.”

What to do: Never pay money for a prize. Ignore, block, and report the message.

💬

Cyberbullying

Cyberbullying means threatening, insulting, embarrassing, or repeatedly troubling someone online.

Example: Someone posts hurtful comments about a student or shares their photo without permission.

What to do: Take a screenshot, block the person, report the account, and tell a trusted adult.

🦠

Malware and Viruses

Unknown applications, unsafe downloads, and suspicious attachments can install harmful software on your device.

Example: A website says, “Download this free game APK now,” then asks for access to contacts and messages.

What to do: Download apps only from official app stores. Do not open unknown files or attachments.

🔓

Identity Theft

Criminals can misuse photos, addresses, documents, phone numbers, and personal details to pretend to be you.

Example: Someone asks for your Aadhaar card photo or school ID “to verify your account.”

What to do: Do not send personal documents to unknown people. Tell a parent, teacher, or trusted adult.
